Areas are ongoing responsibilities — no end date, just a standard to maintain.

## My areas

- [[Areas/Learning & Development|Learning & Development]] — staying sharp, courses, reading
- [[Areas/AI Experiments|AI Experiments]] — ongoing tinkering with AI tools and models

## How areas differ from projects
A project ends. An area doesn't — you just keep maintaining it.  
Example: "Learn Python" is a project. "Stay technically sharp" is an area.
