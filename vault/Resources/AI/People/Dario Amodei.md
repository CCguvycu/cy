# Dario Amodei

**Role:** Co-founder and CEO of Anthropic  
**Previously:** VP of Research at OpenAI

## Why he matters
Leads Anthropic — the company behind Claude. One of the most prominent voices on AI safety and responsible development.

## Key ideas
- AI safety must be taken seriously now, not after AGI
- Scaling laws — believes capability improvements from scale are predictable
- "Powerful AI for the long-term benefit of humanity" — Anthropic's mission

## Notable writing
- **"Machines of Loving Grace"** (2024) — optimistic essay on what a world with beneficial AI could look like
  - Covers: biology/medicine, mental health, economic growth, governance
  - Worth reading for a grounded positive vision
