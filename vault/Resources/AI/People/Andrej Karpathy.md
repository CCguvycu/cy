# Andrej Karpathy

**Role:** Independent researcher, educator  
**Previously:** Director of AI at Tesla, founding member of OpenAI  

## Why he matters
One of the best teachers in AI. His YouTube videos and writing make complex ideas genuinely understandable.

## Best content
- **"The spelled-out intro to neural networks"** — builds a neural net from scratch in Python
- **"Let's build GPT"** — implements a GPT from scratch, explains every step
- **"The unreasonable effectiveness of RNNs"** — classic blog post
- **"State of GPT"** — great overview of how LLMs are trained

## Key ideas he's communicated well
- Backpropagation — intuitive explanations of the maths
- Tokenisation — why it's weirder than it looks
- The gap between pretraining and assistants (SFT + RLHF)
